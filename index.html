About the League

Welcome to The Garage Cup—where golf season doesn’t end when winter arrives. We’re a group of friends bringing friendly competition, big swings, and a few questionable shots to a garage golf simulator.

Our home course? The Shank Shack. Our goal? Earn points, climb the Garage Cup standings, and take home bragging rights. It all leads to The Garage Open, our season-ending championship.
