League Rules

House rules for a fun, fair, and friendly Garage Cup season. The format for each round is listed on the schedule.

Season & points

Six-week season

Each scheduled round contributes to the season standings. Check the schedule for dates, courses, and formats.

Round points

A round win earns each player 200 points. A tie earns each tied player 100 points.

Championship

The season leads to The Garage Open. Championship qualification and scoring can be confirmed by the league before the final round.

Playing formats

Scramble

  1. Everyone on the team tees off.
  2. Choose the team’s best shot; all team members play their next shot from that spot.
  3. Repeat until the ball is holed, then record one team score for the hole.

Alternate shot

  1. Partners play one ball and alternate shots until it is holed.
  2. Partners alternate tee shots; agree who tees off on odd- and even-numbered holes before the round.
  3. Keep the same order throughout each hole, including putts and penalty shots.

Good league etiquette

  • Use the course, tee, and game settings agreed on for the round.
  • Take turns fairly, keep play moving, and let the group know if you need to step away.
  • Call out scoring or simulator issues before moving on to the next hole.
  • Keep it friendly: the goal is good golf, good company, and bragging rights.

These are house-rules and are subject to change. Confirm any simulator settings, handicap use, tie-breakers, and championship details with the group before play.
